I work in cybersecurity, focusing on incident response, threat hunting, and detection engineering.

This site is where I write up things I've worked through - investigations, detection logic, tooling notes. The goal is to document the reasoning, not just the outcome: what the initial signal looked like, which paths turned out to be dead ends, and what would have made the detection faster. Most write-ups are anonymized, with identifying details removed or changed.

I'm interested in the parts of the job that don't fit neatly into a playbook, the noisy alerts, the gaps between what a tool claims to cover and what it actually catches, and the lessons that only surface after an incident is closed.

Posts are mostly in English, occasionally in Turkish.
